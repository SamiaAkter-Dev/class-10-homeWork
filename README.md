## 📖 About The Project

This project is a dedicated portfolio showcase built with semantic HTML5, custom CSS styling, and the Bootstrap 5 responsive framework. It features a modern dark aesthetic with neon green accents, highlighting Shakib Al Hasan's legendary journey from tape-ball cricket in Magura to becoming the world's #1 all-rounder.

---

## 📑 Website Sections

### 1. Hero Showcase
- **Headline:** *"The Tiger Who Does It All"*
- Player introduction, ICC #1 all-rounder highlight tag, and quick-action navigation buttons (`Explore career`, `View records`).

### 2. Quick Stats & About (`01 — ABOUT`)
- **Key Stats:** Summary counters for international runs and matches.
- **Title:** *"From Magura to the World Stage"*
- **Player Profile Table:**
  | Property | Details |
  | :--- | :--- |
  | **Born** | 24 March 1987 · Magura, Bangladesh |
  | **Role** | All-rounder |
  | **Batting** | Left-handed |
  | **Bowling** | Slow left-arm orthodox |
  | **Debut** | ODI & T20I (2006) · Test (2007) |

### 3. Career Milestones (`02 — CAREER`)
*Two Decades, One Journey* — An interactive chronological journey across his iconic career:
- **2006:** International debut in ODIs and T20Is against Zimbabwe.
- **2007:** Test cap and standout performance in Bangladesh's World Cup campaign.
- **2009:** Appointed national captain.
- **2012 & 2014:** IPL Champion with Kolkata Knight Riders (KKR).
- **2015:** Simultaneous ICC World No. 1 all-rounder in Tests, ODIs, and T20Is.
- **2019:** Historic World Cup campaign (606 runs and 11 wickets).

### 4. Career Records (`03 — RECORDS`)
- **Format Breakdown:** Categorized statistical performance across **Test**, **ODI**, and **T20I** cricket.

### 5. Honours & Achievements (`04 — HONOURS`)
*Milestones & Silverware* highlights:
- 🏆 **IPL Champion ×2:** Title winner with Kolkata Knight Riders (2012, 2014).
- 🥇 **ICC No.1 All-Rounder:** Longest-reigning top all-rounder across formats.
- 🇧🇩 **National Captain:** Led Bangladesh across formats over multiple eras.
- ⚡ **7,000 Runs & 300 Wickets:** Rare elite ODI all-rounder double.
- 🌍 **Global T20 Star:** Franchise presence across IPL, BPL, CPL, PSL, and Big Bash.
- 🎯 **2019 World Cup:** 600+ runs and 10+ wickets in a single tournament.

### 6. Photo Gallery (`05 — GALLERY`)
- Curated grid layout titled *"Moments in Frame"* showcasing memorable match photos, fan artwork, and iconic celebrations.

### 7. Media & Booking (`06 — CONTACT`)
- Inquiry form for media, brand sponsorships, coaching clinics, and appearances.
- **Footer:** Fan-made portfolio disclaimer and social links (Instagram, Facebook, X).
